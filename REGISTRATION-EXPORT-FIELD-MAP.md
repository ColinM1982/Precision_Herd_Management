# NSR/CPS Registration Export Field Map

The v0.3.0 litter page produces four CSV exports based on the supplied templates.

## NSR Application for Registration

The export includes owner name/address/contact, herd mark, breeder number, breed, dam and sire names, ear notches and registration numbers, litter number/notch, farrow date, parity, TNB, NBA, LBW, NAT, NW, LWW, weaning date, estrus date, individual pig class/notch, teat counts, weights, registered name/number, and status.

## NSR Breeding Certificate

The export includes certificate date, sow name and registration number, boar name and registration number, breeding date, signature name, boar-owner breeder number, and breed.

## CPS Litter & Individual Recording Application

The export includes breed, individual/litter selection information, owner name/address/contact, herd mark, owner number, litter ear notch, farrow date, dam/sire ear notch and registration number, total born, total born alive, boar/gilt/barrow classification, individual notch, and transfer/sale information.

## CPS Breeding Certificate by Natural Service

The export includes breed, gilt/sow name and registration number, owner name, breeding date, boar registration number, boar-owner number, and service-sire owner/signature name.

## Where the information is entered

| Information | App location |
| --- | --- |
| Owner, address, herd mark, association number, signature | Settings > Registration export details |
| Dam ear notch and registered name | Animals > sow/gilt profile |
| Dam registration number | Animals > sow/gilt profile > Registrations |
| Sire registration, ear notch, owner and owner number | Boar Selection > Add boar |
| Breeding date and method | Reproduction > Record breeding |
| Litter totals and farrow date | Reproduction > Add litter / farrowing |
| Parity, weights, NAT/NW, weaning and estrus | Litters > litter details |
| Individual notch, class, teats, weights and status | Litters > animal row |
| Sale date, price and purchaser | Litters > animal row > Sale info |

CSV output is intended to organize portal entry and provide a reviewable registration record. Association portal requirements and form versions can change, so the user should review each export before submission.
